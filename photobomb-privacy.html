Privacy Policy for PhotoBomb

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

PhotoBomb does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from users.

Photos selected by users are processed locally on the user's device for puzzle creation and are not stored on our servers.

Gameplay information, scores, and settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

If optional sharing or email features are used, those actions occur through external applications on the user's device.
